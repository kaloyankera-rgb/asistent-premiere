# Colour audit: Auto color against what the editors set (2026-11-22)

The user (2026-11-22): Auto color "seems almost not doing anything", only Exposure -0.5 / +0.5 is used. Read only: the colour
learning records of this Mac (`<app>/learning/color/*.jsonl`, ~1,600 unique clip ranges of 10 clients: each clip's Lumetri as
the editor left it, next to what Auto color would have set from the file) and `color_audit.jsonl` (116 Auto color presses).

## Use
116 presses, nearly all between 2026-11-01 and 2026-11-12, one since 2026-11-19. 99 ended CORRECTED; the median written
Exposure was +1.15. Auto color does write values; the user stopped pressing it.

## Exposure: right direction, close on average, noisy per clip
- Where both changed it, Auto color and the editors go the same way in about 99 % of clips.
- Skin-based clips (high confidence, editor corrected, not clamped; editors' units converted to stops at ~0.85 per unit):
  the editors' moves put skin at about 0.59 on average (Asistent aims 0.62); per client 0.53 (Masterhaus), 0.55 (GStroy),
  0.58 (RoboHubPro), 0.59 (ЕКОПАК), 0.67 (Fornetti and the clients misfiled as "Assets"). Mid-tone-based clips: median
  difference -0.08 stop.
- Per clip the correlation with the editors is only 0.3-0.7; within one project and camera Auto color's values spread a little
  more than the editors' (0.36 against 0.29 stop).

## White balance: a coin flip
On the 596 clips where an editor moved Temperature by 5 or more, Auto color went the same way on 248 (42 %), called the clip
balanced on 158 (27 %) and went the opposite way on 185 (31 %). The editors mostly cool the picture (Lumetri Temperature,
middle values: GStroy about -25, ReShape -10, Pulse Fitness -9); within one project and camera Auto color's Temperature spreads
twice as much as the editors' (8.5 against 4.1 units). Auto color makes a found neutral surface grey; the editors do something
else, closer to one value per camera and shoot.

## What the editors change that Auto color cannot
Saturation (110-150 on many Pulse Fitness, GStroy and Fornetti clips) and Contrast (GStroy -25 to -150, REVO +15). The finished
exports (the other files in this folder) also show deep blacks and stronger colour. A correct Exposure and white balance alone
looks like "almost nothing" next to that.

## Faults found in the learning (fixed in 0.10.142)
- A video folder mounted as its own volume filed the clips under the client "Assets" (733 clip ranges of six clients).
- Stills, renders, stock and sound-designed renders were recorded as cameras ("Assets", "renders", "Със SFX").
- Only Auto color's rounded suggestion was kept, not what it measured: targets could not be refitted without the files.
- Only five Lumetri values were read.
- The report compared the editors' Lumetri units with Auto color's stops.
Also to keep in mind: an untouched clip (4a1a Racing 0 %, ЕКОПАК 19 % corrected) is not proof it needed nothing.

The user's decisions (DECISIONS 2026-11-22): learn from all editors; Saturation and Contrast per client allowed; one read-only
pass over the office raw files of the old records. Next: TODO COL (install, re-measure, fit per-client targets judged on held-out
videos, white balance per camera and shoot, Saturation / Contrast).
