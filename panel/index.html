Export check
Checks every export

When an export finishes, Asistent plays the exported file through FFmpeg in the background and reports what it measured: black bars, black and frozen picture, sound on one channel only or silent, true peak above -1 dBFS, full-scale (clipped) samples, silence at the start, and the size, frame rate and length against the sequence. Loudness is shown next to -14 LUFS, the usual platform level, as information only. It only reads the file: nothing is changed. It watches the same folders as Show exports.
