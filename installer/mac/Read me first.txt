Asistent for Premiere Pro: install on a Mac
===========================================

You need: a Mac with Premiere Pro 2026 or newer, and the internet. Nothing else.

1. Quit Premiere Pro if it is open.

2. Double-click "Install Asistent.command".

   The first time, macOS stops it with a message like "Apple could not verify..." or
   "cannot be opened because it is from an unidentified developer". That is normal for a free tool. Then:
   - click Done (or OK), not Move to Trash,
   - open System Settings > Privacy & Security,
   - scroll down to "Install Asistent.command was blocked" and click Open Anyway,
   - enter your Mac password if asked, and click Open Anyway again.
   (On an older macOS: right-click the file, choose Open, then Open.)

3. A Terminal window opens and installs everything by itself (5 to 15 minutes, about 600 MB).
   If macOS asks whether Terminal may access a folder, click Allow.
   Wait until it says "Done".

4. Open Premiere Pro, then Window > UXP Plugins > Asistent.

5. For subtitles: in Asistent's Subtitles card, press the button that downloads the speech model (about 3 GB) once.

New versions install from the Update button at the top of the panel. You do not need this installer again.

If something goes wrong, the window says so. Send a photo of it to the person who gave you Asistent.
